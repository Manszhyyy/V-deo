# «Первая страница» — treatment

Style: **Marker on Paper** (`marker-paper`), adapted: the sheet is a page of a school squared notebook (faint blue grid), lettering in a Cyrillic marker hand (Caveat Bold). Everything else is the style as written: one black marker, blue = what I have, red = what I want.

Format: 1920×1080, 24 fps, ≈ 46 s. Russian first-person voice-over (a 14-year-old), burned-in Russian subtitles on the torn paper slip, `subtitles_ru.srt`.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Comparison until the turn.** Me (a blue "3") against a row of perfect "5"s; my attempts scribbled out one by one; a push through the zero of "0 ₽" into a new sheet where the line starts to climb; end on a peel to a blank page — "это только первая страница".
2. **Countdown of attempts.** Attempt 1, 2, 3… each a sheet, each scribbled out, the 7th works. Clear but repetitive, and it promises a success story the user didn't tell.
3. **Graph of the day.** One long black line drawn through a day (school, homework, night with AI) that dips and rises. Elegant, but the "everyone else is perfect" feeling gets lost.

**Chosen: 1.** It matches what the user said — sad start, honest "I'm an ordinary C-student", and the energy of trying on his own with AI — without inventing a big win. The ending is not money, it's the decision to continue: the blank page.

## Logline and arc

A 14-year-old C-student watches everyone around him succeed, keeps failing to earn his first money, and decides that this is only the first page.

- Setup (0–15 s): who I am — a blue "3"; everyone else — a row of "5".
- Struggle (15–27 s): "как заработать?" — attempts scribbled out, a red "0 ₽". Near-silence.
- Turn (27–38 s): push through the "0" into a new sheet: me + laptop + "ИИ"; a staircase drawn step by step.
- Ending (38–46 s): "я не сдаюсь" — red goal at the top; peel to a blank squared page with only the page number "1".

## Benchmark

- Hand-drawn personal explainer videos where one person talks to camera through drawings on paper, and diary-style animated shorts. **Learn**: one idea per sheet, honesty of the hand, quiet moments. **Don't take**: any drawings, characters, layouts, melodies.

## Voice-over (Russian)

1. Мне четырнадцать. Я обычный троечник.
2. Смотрю на других — у всех всё получается. Все учатся на отлично.
3. А я просто пытаюсь заработать. Сам.
4. Пробую одно, другое… и ничего не выходит.
5. Но у меня есть голова, руки — и ИИ.
6. Я учусь каждый день. Шаг за шагом.
7. Я пока не отличник. Но я не сдаюсь.
8. Это только первая страница.
