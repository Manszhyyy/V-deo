# «Мост» — трейлер (treatment)

Style: **Low-poly Isometric Island** (`lowpoly-island`): flat-shaded pastel 3D, orthographic isometric camera, everything arrives with a bounce and a note.
Format: 1920×1080, 24 fps, ≈ 45 s. Narrator (Gemini TTS, voice «Kore», third person), subtitles without a box, the title rising like tiles.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Two worlds join.** Two islands, two villages that haven't crossed in a hundred years. A boy and a girl start a bridge from each shore; a storm breaks it overnight; in the morning both villages come out and finish it together; dusk, lanterns, people crossing. A real arc with a real result.
2. **Erosion.** A finished town loses pieces one by one to the sea. Sad, and the result is only loss.
3. **A year in four seasons** on one island. Pretty, but no stakes.

**Chosen: 1** — the style's own "two worlds side by side trading pieces across a gap until they join", and different from the library demo (one island growing from an empty sea).

## Logline

Two islands that haven't spoken in a hundred years — until two kids start building a bridge, one from each side.

## Arc

- Hook (0–2.5 s): close on the water between two shores; *plonk* — a single plank tile shoots up with a splash and a note. Pull out by zoom: two islands, far apart, a gap of sea.
- Setup (2.5–10 s): «Два острова. Сто лет никто не плавал друг к другу.» Turntable over the left island (coral roofs) and the right island (blue roofs); fishermen turn their backs.
- The kids (10–20 s): «Пока двое детей не начали строить мост. Каждый — со своей стороны.» A boy pushes planks from the left, a girl from the right; planks drop and stack on pontoons, each a note (the left notes and the right notes answer each other); days pass by the sun sweeping; the halves reach toward each other.
- Storm (20–26 s): night, rain, waves; «Шторм сломал всё за одну ночь.» Planks lift and tumble into the sea; music to near zero.
- Morning (26–34 s): dawn; the kids alone on the broken stubs… then doors open on both islands, people walk to the shores. «А утром на берег вышли все.» Planks drop in fast succession from both sides — the build accelerates by density; the last plank lands in the middle on the downbeat.
- Title (34–38 s): «МОСТ» letters rise from the water.
- Button (38–45 s): dusk, lanterns light along the bridge one by one (emissives), the boy and the girl meet in the middle, people cross both ways.

## Score and sound

Event-driven: every plank is a note (marimba on the left, kalimba on the right, answering), soft pad and light shaker; the storm thins to a low drone and digital zero before the morning; the final plank lands on the big downbeat; lanterns are celesta/glockenspiel. Foley: splashes and bubble sweeps for rising pieces, wooden knocks for planks, rain and wind at night, gulls by day, a crowd murmur on the morning shore. No piano, no strings.
