# What the popular Russian thriller/horror Shorts do (sample from YouTube search, Nov 2026)

Method: 25 Russian queries (триллер/страшная история/хоррор/мистика + анимация/shorts), ~1100 results; the 48 most-viewed
thriller-type Shorts (1.2–23 M views) compared from three frames each (25 / 50 / 75 % of the video, YouTube's public thumbnails).
Full pages and the videos themselves could not be opened from this environment, so sound and exact timing were not measured.

| Element | What the top Shorts do | Applied in «Ночная смена» |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Close, vertical, perspective; faces fill the frame; low angles; slow push-ins | Perspective camera, close-ups, slow push-ins, slight handheld drift |
| Light & shadows | Dark, one light source, hard shadows, cold blue/teal + one warm or red accent | Monitor glow (blue), desk lamp (warm, hard shadow), red door lamp, green emergency lights |
| Image | Night-vision / CCTV / found-footage look, grain, vignette | CCTV feeds rendered live on the monitors and full screen, grain, scanlines, rec dot |
| Characters | Semi-realistic 3D or simple 2D; exaggerated fear on faces; a "wrong" figure staring at the camera | Rounded 3D people, big eyes, fear expressions; a man among faceless mannequins with eye-shine |
| Subtitles | 1–2 words at a time, big, centre screen, white + one highlighted word | 1–2 words, 104 px, centre, the spoken word in yellow |
| Story format | Second person («ты»), "rules" / creepypasta («Никогда не…», «Правила ночной смены»), a twist at the end | Three rules of a night shift, each broken; ends on a cliff-hanger + A/Б choice |
| Hook | Scariest image or a forbidden rule in the first 1–2 s | Opens on the CCTV feed with «нельзя смотреть в камеры» and a one-frame flash of the man |
