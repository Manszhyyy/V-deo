# «Потерянная звезда» — трейлер (treatment)

Style: **Firefly Pointillism** (`firefly-pointillism`). Thousands of warm points on black; one pool of particles for the whole film.
Format: 1920×1080, 60 fps, 45 s. A trailer for an imagined animated film. Russian narrator (Gemini TTS, voice «Charon»), title cards made of points, burned-in subtitles.

Made after «Последний свет»: same world and animation, a new story with no paradox — a star fell, it is lost, a boy takes it home.

## Logline

One night a small star falls into a sleeping city — and a boy carries it up to the highest roof to send it home.

## Arc

- Hook (0–2 s): a bright streak falls from the top right into the street; a deep impact; a ripple through the windows.
- Setup (2–12 s): the star floats up, scared. Card «ОНА УПАЛА С НЕБА». A boy gathers from the embers, looks up, the star lands on his palm. Voice: «Маленькая. Испуганная. Совсем одна.»
- Quest (12–27 s): «Чтобы вернуть её домой, нужно подняться выше всех.» Card «ЕЙ НУЖНО ДОМОЙ». Street → bridge (reflection) → storm («Даже если на пути гроза»), the star nearly goes out.
- Roof (27–30 s): near-silence; he lifts it.
- Home (30–33 s): it flies up into the sky; a flash ripples through every point; the whole sleeping city wakes, every window lights.
- Title (33–38 s): windows swap into «ПОТЕРЯННАЯ ЗВЕЗДА».
- Button (38–45 s): the star twinkles back at him. «Иногда даже звёздам нужен друг.» «СКОРО».

## Voice-over

1. 0.5 s — Однажды ночью с неба упала звезда.
2. 7.9 s — Маленькая. Испуганная. Совсем одна.
3. 12.25 s — Чтобы вернуть её домой, нужно подняться выше всех.
4. 22.4 s — Даже если на пути гроза.
5. 39.2 s — Иногда даже звёздам нужен друг.

All five lines come from one Gemini request (tools/tts_gemini_one.py), split at the pauses — the free tier allows few requests a day.

Shot list, cue map and sound design are those of «Последний свет» (../posledniy-svet/TREATMENT.md) with the blackout replaced by the fall and impact, and the relit city by a sleeping city that wakes on the flash.
