# «Последний свет» — трейлер (treatment)

Style: **Firefly Pointillism** (`firefly-pointillism`). Thousands of warm points on black; one pool of particles for the whole film.
Format: 1920×1080, 60 fps (the style's rate), ≈ 45 s. A trailer for an imagined animated film. Russian narrator, few lines; title cards made of points; burned-in subtitles.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Blackout → quest → release.** A whole night city of window lights goes out in one wave in the first second. One green light survives. A boy finds it; the rule: let it go into the sky and the city lights up again — but only once. Montage of the journey (street, bridge, storm), a near-silence on the roof, release, the whole city flashes back on, the title gathers from the windows. Button: the boy alone, a new green star.
2. **A list that turns.** «Он боялся темноты. Он боялся высоты. Он боялся остаться один.» — each fear a scene; the last card turns: «Этой ночью ему придётся всё сразу». Clear trailer grammar but the images become illustrations of words.
3. **Countdown to dawn.** 03:00 … 05:59: the boy must reach the hill before sunrise. Tension, but a clock fights the soft, breathing medium.

**Chosen: 1.** Its hook is pure image — a city going dark — readable at thumbnail size with no words, and it spends the medium's best moves where a trailer needs them: *going out* as the hook, *the guiding light* as the hero's companion, *gust change of scene* for montage cuts, *synchronous flash* as the money shot, *identity swap* for the title.

## Logline and arc

In a city that went dark in one night, a boy carries the last light to the highest roof — to give it away.

- Hook (0–4.5 s): lit city → blackout wave → one green light breathing in the dark.
- Setup (4.5–16 s): card «КОГДА ГАСНЕТ ВСЁ»; the boy gathers out of the embers; the light lands on his palm; the rule (voice).
- Stakes + montage (16–27 s): card «НО ТОЛЬКО ОДИН РАЗ»; gusts cut street run → bridge over the river (reflection) → storm (head wind, the light nearly goes out).
- Breath (27–29 s): the roof, near-silence, he lifts the light.
- Release (29–33 s): it flies up; a synchronous flash ripples across the skyline; every window relights.
- Title (33–38 s): the windows swap identity into «ПОСЛЕДНИЙ СВЕТ».
- Button (38–45 s): dark again, the boy small on the roof; one new green star; card «СКОРО».

## Benchmark

Animated-feature teaser trailers (one image hook, two or three cards, a music build, a single big reveal, a quiet button). **Learn**: card rhythm, the drop to silence before the reveal, a button that makes you smile. **Don't take**: any characters, shots, music or type.

## Cue map

Trailer build in D major with a Lydian G♯ colour. 0–0.9 a high string shimmer; 0.9 a sub boom + cymbal choke on the blackout, then silence with crickets; 2.2 a glass-harp note for the green light (two-note rising motif = question); 4.6 harp arpeggio on the card; 7.6 strings enter, flute motif on the meeting; 12 timpani pulse starts, tempo 96; 16 hit on the card; 18.8–26.8 build: ostinato strings, timpani every beat, cymbal swells on each gust; 26.8 drop to near-silence (rain fades, one heartbeat); 29.2 harp glissando up with the light; 30.4 synchronous flash = full orchestral D major hit + harp sweep; 33 title on the resolved chord, held; 38 solo glockenspiel motif answer (falling to the tonic) on the new star; 43 last chime on «СКОРО».

## Titles and subtitles

Cards and title are made of points (Cormorant Garamond SemiBold, OFL, Cyrillic), left-aligned in empty sky, frozen after landing. Subtitles: Cormorant Garamond Medium ≈ 44 px warm cream, centred at the bottom, faint glow, no plate.
