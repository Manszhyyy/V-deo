# «НЕ NPC» — трейлер (treatment)

Style: **Firefly Pointillism** (`firefly-pointillism`), the same world of points as «Последний свет» / «Потерянная звезда».
Format: 1920×1080, 60 fps, 45 s. Russian narrator (Gemini TTS «Charon»), cards and title made of points, burned-in subtitles.

A trending premise — simulation theory, "the world is a game and everyone else is an NPC" — told in a medium where people
literally are made of points of light.

## Logline

At 3 a.m. the whole world hangs. Only one boy keeps moving — and he has one minute to find the exit before the world deletes itself.

## Arc

- Hook (0–2.4 s): a night street, a crowd of passers-by (cool teal points) walking; the boy among them (warm amber). At 2.4 s
  everything freezes mid-step with a digital glitch (bands of points jump sideways), the street's sound stops dead.
  Only he moves — stops, looks round. Voice: «В три часа ночи весь мир завис.»
- Card «МИР ЗАВИС». He walks among the frozen; touches one man — who blows apart into points. «Все вокруг оказались ненастоящими.»
- Card «КРОМЕ НЕГО». «Кроме него.»
- The run (14.6–24.6 s): the world starts deleting from the left — buildings, street, frozen people lift away as points;
  he runs ahead of the front. «У него есть минута, чтобы найти выход.»
- The roof (24.6–28.4 s): a door of light gathers in the sky from the stars. Near-silence, a heartbeat.
- The leap (28.4–29.7 s): he jumps into the door. Flash: the world reloads — the street, the windows, the crowd walking again.
- Title «НЕ NPC».
- Twist (38.6–45 s): one passer-by in the crowd stops… looks up… and turns warm — alive. «Он думал, что он единственный.» «СКОРО».

## Voice-over

3. 12.1 s — Кроме него.

5. 39.4 s — Он думал, что он единственный.

## Sound

Music: D minor, 96 bpm spiccato pulse that cuts dead on the freeze; a hanging drone and high glass; a falling glockenspiel run on
the touch; the build (ostinato, timpani, horns) under the run; bowed vibraphone for the door; harp up on the leap; the only
D major chord on the reload; everything stops for the twist. Foley: crowd footsteps and street murmur (stop with the freeze),
a digital stutter and sub drop, electric hum of a hung world, crumbling ticks of the deletion, gusts, whooshes.
