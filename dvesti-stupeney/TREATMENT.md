# «Двести ступеней» — трейлер (treatment)

Style: **Woodcut Print** (`woodcut`). Black wood, light only where the knife cut; one colour plate — a burnt copper-orange — reserved for the fire of the lighthouse.
Format: 1920×1080, 24 fps, ≈ 45 s. A trailer for an imagined animated film. Russian narrator (few lines, low and plain), letterpress captions in the margin, the title carved.

## Three candidate structures

1. **The light dies, a child brings it back.** Storm; the lighthouse goes out; her father is at sea; she climbs two hundred steps and lights it; the beam finds his boat. Clear stakes, a ticking clock (the storm, the rocks), one colour with a perfect arc: it dies in the first second and returns in her hands.
2. **A book of hours.** Seven locked plates, 21:00 → 05:00, one per hour of the night. Beautiful but slow for a trailer.
3. **Two blocks printed in turn.** The boat and the girl alternate impressions until one sheet holds both. Strong form, but the climb loses its momentum.

**Chosen: 1**, cut with the energy of (3): sea and stairs alternate on hard cuts as the music builds.

## Logline

In the night the lighthouse goes dark, a ten-year-old girl climbs two hundred steps through the storm — because her father's boat is still out there.

## Benchmark

Wordless woodcut novels for plate-by-plate storytelling and the gouge as emotion; animated-feature teaser trailers for card rhythm, a drop to silence before the reveal and a quiet button. Copy no plates, figures, borders, titles, music.

## Cue map

D minor, 100 bpm. 0–2.3: a low drone, the beam's hum; thunder + gran cassa on the blackout, then silence and rain. Plate 2–3: cellos spiccato ostinato, log drum. Card: a struck-metal tone. The climb: ostinato + timpani on every cut, rising by step, the counter on the downbeats. Plate 6: tam-tam. Plate 7: true silence before the match — the match strike is the first sound. Plate 8: the full orchestra on the first ray, strings open to D major. Title: a held chord and the peel's paper sounds. Button: a solo low string melody resolving, gulls, soft surf.
